## About section — Story (max 5,000 characters)

```
SabiWabi is a small studio for people who love characters — the kind of odd, charming little figures that make a desk or a shelf feel like yours.

We design original characters using AI as a creative tool, then refine and prepare each one as a clean, print-ready 3D model. The name comes from wabi-sabi, the idea that warmth and personality matter more than flawless polish. That is the feeling we go for: figurines with a bit of soul.

What you get
Every listing is an instant digital download — no shipping, no waiting. Files come print-ready as STL (for FDM and resin printing) and GLB (textured, for digital use, AR, and rendering). Print them at home, send them to a print service, or drop them into a game or scene.

How it is made
Each character starts as an AI-assisted concept and is then turned into a 3D model, checked, and prepped for printing. We believe in being upfront: these designs are created with the help of AI tools. What you are buying is a finished, usable, original character file.

Who it is for
Hobby printers building a collection, gift-givers after something a little different, tabletop players, and makers who just like having tiny friends around. New characters are added regularly.

A note on printing
Results depend on your printer, settings, and finishing. We design with printability in mind, but a little tuning is part of the fun. Questions before you buy? Message us — we are happy to help.

Thanks for stopping by. — SabiWabi
```
